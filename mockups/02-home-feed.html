About the Guild

We are a friendly group who exist to encourage and support anyone who loves textiles — design, colour, texture, or creating and experimenting with yarn and fibres through weaving, spinning and dyeing. We are keen to encourage beginners as well as the more experienced, and to share ideas, skills, knowledge and enthusiasm.

The Guild was formed in 1958, and we have maintained a healthy membership throughout that time. We are affiliated to the National Association of the Guilds of Weavers, Spinners and Dyers — all our members automatically become members of the National Association.

Committee: Anne Guyon (Chair); Carol Houghton (Secretary); Claudette Davies (Treasurer); Sue Browne; Annette Ford; Mim Barnes; Anna Aires; Eleonora Knowland.

Meetings & what we do

We hold monthly meetings with invited speakers, locally and nationally recognised, on a range of textile topics — plus workshops, Open Weave and Spin days, exhibitions and visits.

We meet on the third Thursday of the month, 1–4pm (second Thursday in December). Open Weave and Spin days run on four Saturdays a year, 10am–3pm, and are open to drop-ins.
