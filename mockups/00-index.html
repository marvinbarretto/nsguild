Redesign mockups — round 1

Low-res layout explorations. Real content, honest hierarchy, shared refined-handmade style baseline (baseline.css). Each page ends with a yellow notes box listing the decisions it embodies.

01 · Homepage — two columns

Next event promoted to a full-width strip; news and upcoming programme side by side; static guild info moved below anchor chips; gallery polaroids as a strip.

02 · Homepage — merged feed

Same top structure, but news + events interleaved in one labelled chronological feed, with polaroids running down the side rail.

03 · Equipment — single stacked page

Full cards inline (photo, price, location, contact); anchors + copy-link keep items shareable; friendly empty state for the loan section.

Round 2 — imagery experiments on 01

Iterations on the two-column layout: imagery moved above the fold, next event toned down to an informational panel, and everything shown derivable from real CMS fields (title, date, prose, photos).

04 · Washing line

Polaroids pegged on a string right under the nav — wonky and handmade; scrolls sideways on mobile.

05 · Fabric ribbon

Edge-to-edge band of photos with pinked (zigzag) edges, like a strip of cloth — boldest colour hit for the least height.

06 · Hero mosaic

Photo mosaic and the next-event panel share the top row — biggest imagery presence, most conventional layout.

Round 3 — ribbon in motion

07 · Animated fabric ribbon

The 05 ribbon with motion: a slow seamless drift (pauses on hover), a shuffle mode where photos crossfade in place, and a live toggle to compare — reduced-motion falls back to still.
